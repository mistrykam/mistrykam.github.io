### Unlock Your Mind

A summary from a live event given by Marczell Klein. These were his main points:

1. **You need to take responsibility for your life.** You cannot blame others for your current circumstances.  You cannot act like a victim blaming your upbringing, your teachers, your bosses, your family, your friends, the government, the one percent, or lack of resources. You are the driver of your life, you are not being driven around.
2. **Our mind consists of two parts: conscious mind (pilot) vs subconscious mind (autopilot).** The subconscious mind runs 95% of the time, it controls your breathing, heartbeat, blood flow, etc. It also contains your long-term memories, beliefs, identities, habits, patterns and programs. This represents your model of the world. These beliefs and programs were first installed when we were younger and they direct our behaviour.  Good news is they can be changed.
3. **The majority of the time we are running in autopilot mode, our  mind focusing on past or future events.** Our conscious mind has limited capacity to ingest information, it is easily overwhelmed, which is why the subconscious mind takes over.  We can use pattern interrupts to break out of it, to remain present. By leveraging trance states we can change our beliefs, programs and memories. We can install empowering beliefs and delete negative memories. 
4. **Blueprint: Our expectations about the future events form our reality.** Have you planned what you will do in the next few days, the following week, month, the next 90 days, or in one year from now. Categories: health, wealth, relationships, clothes, shelter, transportation, items you want, places you want to travel to, experiences you want to have. What is it currently like, be real, what's your expectations? Audit what you did in the past days, week, month and year. Ask: What would you like to happen? How will I make it happen? What little thing I could do today? Asking an empowering question: What can I do today to have incredible health, wealth and relationships.
5. **Identify your limiting beliefs about health, wealth, relationships.** These limiting beliefs act as potholes that slow you down/stop your intentions. By bring these limitation into your consciousness, you can ask yourself if they are actually true. The root of the limiting beliefs is you saying “I am not good enough”. Beliefs don't exist outside your mind, you are good enough to do anything.
6. **Confidence is about how certain you are, certain to succeed or certain to fail, either way you are confident.** Confidence is how comfortable you are doing something, the more you practice (develop the skill-set), the more comfortable you are and hence confident.
7. **Get into peak state, be energized and magnetic.** Take actions to acquire items or be places/environments that energize you. The more energetic you are, the more you can accomplish. Create an anchor to trigger a peak state. Being resourceful is the ultimate state.
8. **List out your values (emotions) you want to have, get clarity.** For example: Freedom, love, joy. Elicit your values by asking “what is the positive intent of having or doing that?“ and “what does having or doing something give you?”
9. **List out your goals (means to your values), what does next week, month, year look like.** Include visual, auditory, and kinesthetic attributes. Be as detailed as possible and give a timeline. Figure out one tiny step you can take towards your goal. (Do one push-up, read one line in a book, make a task list).
10. **List out your fears (ones you can control vs. ones you cannot).** When you consciously review them, they are not as scary as you thought they were. You can safely ignore any fears you can't control. Fears are just thoughts and beliefs, they don't exist in the physical world. Our two-millionaire brain is wired for survival and is always trying to protect us, if the action we want to take does not affect our survival or harm someone, then you can take the action.
11. **Motivation: Pain vs Pleasure - leverage it to move towards your goals.**  Associate pain of not completing your goal and massive pleasure to achieving your goal. Using pain as motivation is like running away from fire, once you're out of danger you lose your momentum.  This is why our goal should be attached to massive pleasure, for example achieving financial freedom for your family so that they can live in abundance.
12. **Frame of reference, many ways to see the same thing.** Our beliefs, values and current state filter how we view the world. Imagine how you would view the world as a top 1%er versus someone in the bottom 10%. Always remember to respect a person's frame/model of the world, use language to shift their frame.
13. **Abundance vs scarcity mindset**  When you are in a state of abundance you feel limitless, you can do anything and help others. When you are in a scarcity state you’ll focus on protecting what little you have, not willing to help others. Imagine owning a Costco, full with food and consumer goods vs scraping by living in a one room basement apartment with an empty fridge. Latter situation we help nobody, the former we help thousands.  We live in an abundant world, there are opportunities everywhere, we need to be a problem solver.
14. **Serve without intentions and attract value (don't chase it).** For example it's impossible to herd cats, they won't be forced to comply, however if you offer delicious tuna in a comfortable setting you can easily attract them. Be the problem solver, find the options and the shortest steps to execute them.
